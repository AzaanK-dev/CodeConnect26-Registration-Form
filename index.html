Build What Matters.
The Vibe Coding Hackathon is the flagship web development competition of CODE//CONNECT'26 at UBIT, University of Karachi, held as part of Career Connect'26. Bring your ideas, sharpen your skills, and turn real-world problems into working solutions.

Compete. Build. Stand Out. Take on real challenges, showcase your skills, gain hands-on industry exposure, and compete for cash prizes, internships, recognition, networking and career opportunities.
Turn your ideas into impact. Turn your skills into opportunities.



HOW IT WORKS:
Online Phase. Pick 1 of 3 problem domains, build your MVP within the time limit, and submit your live link + GitHub repo.

Onsite Phase. Every registered team gets a free entry to Career Connect'26 at UBIT. The Top 10 teams will pitch live on stage, and the winning team takes home the Grand Prize.

Not in the Top 10? You still gain. Explore Career Connect'26, network with software companies and startups, and earn a certificate.
