Build What Matters.
CODE//CONNECT'26 is a high-energy tech competition at UBIT, University of Karachi, held as part of Career Connect'26. Bring your ideas, sharpen your skills, and turn real-world problems into working solutions.

Compete. Build. Stand Out. Take on real challenges, showcase your skills, gain hands-on industry exposure, and compete for cash prizes, internships, recognition, networking and career opportunities.
Turn your ideas into impact. Turn your skills into opportunities.



MODULES:

Hackathon (Online): Choose 1 of 3 problem domains, build an MVP/solution within a fixed time, and submit your live link + GitHub repo. Selected teams may proceed to an on-campus pitch.

Competitive Programming (Onsite): Solve DSA/problem-solving questions under time pressure. Marks are based on questions solved.
